The short version

Refstay gives hosts a personal referral link. When guests use the link and book Miami activities, hosts earn 5% commission, paid monthly. To stay in good standing, don't spam, don't fake bookings, and don't break local laws. We can change the commission rate with 30 days' notice. Either side can end the relationship anytime.

1. Who can use Refstay

You can sign up as a host if:

  • You're 18 or older
  • You operate a short-term rental in Miami, FL or Punta Cana, Dominican Republic (other zones may be added later)
  • You agree to these Terms and our Privacy Policy
  • You're not on a sanctions list or otherwise prohibited from doing business with US-based companies

2. How the commission works

Commission rate

You earn 5% of the booking total (excluding taxes and fees) for any Miami activity your guest books through your personal referral link. We can change this rate with 30 days' written notice; bookings made before the change use the old rate.

Attribution

A booking is attributed to you when:

  • Your guest clicks your refstay.com/r/yourslug link
  • They complete a paid booking at an activity operator we list (currently 538 operators across Miami and Punta Cana)
  • FareHarbor records your slug in the booking reference
  • The booking is not canceled, refunded, or charged back

Payouts

  • Paid monthly by the 15th, for the prior month's confirmed bookings
  • Minimum payout: $25. Balances under $25 roll over.
  • Payment via PayPal or Zelle (you choose in your dashboard)
  • If we can't reach you and a payout is pending for 12 months, the balance is forfeited

What's NOT included

  • Bookings made directly with operators outside Refstay
  • Self-bookings (e.g., you booking your own activities)
  • Bookings that get refunded — we deduct refunded commissions from your balance
  • Booking modifications that lower the total — commission adjusts accordingly
