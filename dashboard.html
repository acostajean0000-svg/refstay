Common questions

How often are my stats updated?

Bookings and earnings refresh monthly when we reconcile from FareHarbor (around the 1st–3rd of each month). Click counts refresh weekly. We'll move to real-time tracking once we cross 25 active hosts.

How do I know my link is working?

Open your link in a private/incognito browser tab. You should land on miamijetskiboatrentals.com normally. To verify the slug is captured, open DevTools console and type window.__refstay.getRef() — it should return your slug.

What if a guest books on a partner site instead of miamijetskiboatrentals.com?

Use a Direct link (see the "Direct links" tab). Those have your slug baked into the URL itself, so they work on any partner site, Instagram bio, WhatsApp message, anywhere a URL goes.

How do I get paid?

PayPal or Zelle, whichever you prefer. Update your payout method in Settings. Payments go out by the 5th of every month for the prior month's bookings.

Printing tips

Paper recommendations

Welcome card / Wall poster / Welcome book: Print on glossy or matte photo paper for premium feel ($15 for 50 sheets on Amazon).
Fridge magnets: Buy "printable magnetic paper" on Amazon ($12 for 5 sheets) OR print on regular paper, laminate, and use double-sided tape with a small magnet on the back.

Print settings

In the print dialog: set "Background graphics" to ON (so the colors print), choose "Letter" or "5×7 photo" depending on the asset, and set margins to "None" or "Minimum". The CSS handles the rest.

Verify your QR works before printing

Click "Open print preview" → scan the QR with your phone's camera before hitting print. It should open refstay.com/r/your-slug. If it doesn't, refresh the preview.
