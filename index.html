FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do I get paid?+
Payouts happen on the 1st of every month via Stripe or PayPal, as long as your balance is above $25. You can track pending and paid balances in your dashboard.
Who counts as a referral?+
Anyone who clicks your unique link and completes a booking within 30 days. Cookies, device fingerprinting, and email matching all count — even if they come back later through a different device.
Is there a cost to join?+
No. The platform is 100% free for hosts. You keep a flat 5% of every booking — no subscription, no platform tax, no fees deducted from your payout.
Why a flat 5%? Don't bigger hosts deserve more?+
Tiered programs reward hosts who already have the biggest audience and punish everyone else. We think a host with one great listing deserves the same rate as a property manager with fifty. Plus, the math is easier — you always know what you're earning.
Can I refer other hosts, not just guests?+
Yes. Hosts you refer get a $50 sign-up bonus, and you earn 5% of their referral commissions for 12 months. Stack the income.
What if a booking is canceled?+
Commissions are confirmed once the guest checks in. Cancellations before check-in are removed from your pending balance — but anything paid out stays yours.
Is my data safe?+
We only collect what's needed to send payouts and track referrals. Your information is never sold or shared with third parties. We're SOC 2 compliant and GDPR-ready.
